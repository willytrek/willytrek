## Research Interests

My research focuses on developing efficient and rapid computational perception systems across spatial scales—from macroscopic environments to microscopic structures—and across representations from 2D to 3D.

- Computational imaging
- Event-based vision
- Computational photography
- Computational microscopy
- 3D imaging and machine perception
